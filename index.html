Questions

The honest answers.

Is this actually different from Spotify?
Structurally, yes. Spotify pools all subscriber money globally and divides it by total streams, so your $12 mostly funds whoever is biggest that month. PHX allocates your pass against your own listening only, and shows you the itemized result. The trade-off is honest: our catalog is Phoenix, not the world.
What does an artist actually get?
50% of every member's pass is allocated to the artists that member streamed, weighted by listening. Artists keep their masters, their merch revenue, and their audience. There is no exclusivity and no term — an artist can be on PHX and everywhere else at the same time.
Why is it invite-only right now?
Because a music platform with more members than artists is a bad experience for both. We're onboarding artists and members in step so the first receipt anyone gets has real names on it.
What happens to my founding rate?
It stays at your founding price for as long as your membership stays continuously active. If you cancel and come back after founding numbers are gone, you rejoin at public pricing.
I'm an artist. How do I get on?
Applications are open and reviewed by hand. You need to be from or based in the Phoenix metro, own or control your masters, and have at least one finished release. Apply from inside the app.
