FAQ

Straight answers.

Where does my $15 actually go?
To the artists you stream, split by how much you listened to each of them — the rest keeps the platform and city perks running. Your receipt shows every dollar that reached artists.
What if I barely stream one month?
Your money still supports Phoenix artists — anything your own listening didn't route goes to artists across the scene instead of disappearing.
Is this only music?
Music is live now. Events, Eats, Cuts and Drops perks join your pass through 2026 at no extra cost.
How do artists get paid?
Monthly, net-60, no minimum threshold. Earnings come only from the members who actually play them.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Your founding number stays reserved for 90 days if you come back.
I'm an artist — how do I get on?
Apply in the app. Listing is free, review takes 24–48 hours, and you keep the rights to your music.
