Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account data: email, username, display name, profile info you add.
  • Content you post: posts, comments, photos and video clips you choose to upload, playlists, liked songs, favorites, and follows. Capturing media happens two ways, both under your control: your device's own camera flow, or the optional in-app PHX Lens camera. PHX Lens asks for camera and microphone access separately: the camera prompt appears when you open the Lens, and the microphone prompt appears only the first time you record a video (photos never involve the microphone). Both run only while the Lens is open on screen and stop the instant you close it. The live camera feed is processed on your device and is never transmitted, recorded, or stored unless you press the shutter — and what you capture is attached to your draft post for you to review before anything is shared.
  • Usage data: streams (which tracks, when, 30-second qualification), likes, comments, follows, session and device identifiers, approximate device characteristics (screen size, timezone, language), and how you arrived (referral code, link source) — used to credit whoever invited you.
  • Direct Messages: the messages you send and receive, stored so your conversations work across devices. See "Direct Messages" below for exactly who can see them.
  • Location (optional): only if you tap "Near me" on the Plug Map, your device asks for permission and your coordinates are used on your device to sort nearby events. PHX does not store your location, build a location history, or share coordinates with anyone.
  • Payment data: handled by Stripe; PHX never stores full card numbers.

Direct Messages

DMs are private. We built the boundaries in from day one, learning from where other platforms got this wrong:

  • Only the two participants can read a conversation. Messages are never shown to other members, never used for advertising, never fed into recommendations, and never used to train AI systems.
  • Automated safety check at send time only: the same community-guidelines filter that screens public posts screens messages as they're sent. It blocks the message or lets it through — it does not build a profile of you.
  • Human review happens in exactly one case: a participant reports the conversation. A PHX administrator can then view recent messages in that conversation to act on the report — and every such review is itself permanently logged and auditable. No report, no human eyes. Our internal operations tooling is technically walled off from message content.
  • Your controls: block any member (stops messages both ways, instantly, no notification to them) and report any conversation. Moderators can freeze a conversation that violates the rules.

Service providers & embeds

Your data is processed by the providers that run PHX: Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage), Vercel (hosting), Resend (transactional email such as confirmations and invite codes), and Stripe (payments, once billing is live). Music videos on artist pages are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode — playing one is subject to YouTube's own terms and privacy policy. We use our own first-party analytics; no third-party advertising trackers run in the app.

How we use it

  • Operating the service — playback, feeds, notifications you request.
  • Calculating artist compensation from listening activity.
  • Personalizing recommendations (your listening history shapes your For You results). A non-personalized "Latest" feed option is always available.
  • Fraud prevention and community safety.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share listening data with advertisers. Artists see aggregate stats (stream counts, listener counts) — never your identity tied to your listening.

Your choices

You can request account deletion and data export by contacting privacy@thephx.app. Push notifications are opt-in and can be disabled in your browser/device settings anytime.

Agreed electronically each time an artist submits a track ("clickwrap"). Version 1.0 — July 2026.

1. Grant of Rights

You grant PHX a non-exclusive, worldwide license to reproduce, transcode, publicly perform, and stream the submitted recordings and associated artwork/metadata on the Platform. You keep full ownership of your masters and compositions. You may distribute your music anywhere else — PHX never demands exclusivity.

2. Your Warranties

You represent that you own or control all rights needed to grant this license (including features, samples, and production), and that submissions contain no infringing, unlawful, or falsely-attributed material. You will indemnify PHX against claims arising from a breach of these warranties.

3. Review & Removal

Submissions are reviewed before going live (typically 24–48 hours). PHX may decline or remove content that violates the Community Rules, this Agreement, or applicable law, and will honor valid DMCA takedowns. You may remove your catalog at any time with 7 days' notice; earned-but-unpaid royalties survive removal.

4. Compensation

Artists earn monthly compensation derived from the qualifying streams (30+ continuous seconds) of the listeners who play their music, as measured by PHX's systems. Earnings for each calendar month are calculated after month close and finalize and pay out 60 days after the month ends (net-60) — this window protects the pool from payment reversals, chargebacks, and streaming fraud. There is no minimum payout threshold. PHX's internal compensation formula may evolve; PHX will report your streams, listeners, and earnings transparently in the artist dashboard.

5. Streaming Fraud

Streams that PHX reasonably determines to be artificial (bots, scripts, coordinated manipulation, self-streaming schemes) do not earn compensation, may be clawed back if already credited, and may result in catalog removal and account termination.

6. Taxes

Artists are independent parties, not employees. You are responsible for your own taxes; PHX will collect a W-9 (or W-8 series) before releasing payouts and will issue 1099s where required by U.S. law.

7. Term & Termination

This Agreement continues per-track while your music remains on the Platform. Sections 2, 4 (accrued amounts), 5, and 6 survive termination. Arizona law governs.
