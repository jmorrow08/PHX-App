The formula, step by step

For EACH paying member, each month: wallet = 50% × what they paid Native $15 → $7.50 · Insider $29 → $14.50 rate = MIN( 0.75¢ , wallet ÷ their counted streams ) artist gets = rate × streams THAT member played of THAT artist leftover = wallet − everything drawn → goes to the COMMUNITY POT The POT at month end: 50% → free-tier fund — pays artists for Explorer (free) streams, capped at 100 streams per free user per artist, same 0.75¢ ceiling 50% → platform

So yes — it's your instinct with two safety valves: 50% of the member's fee, divided across the artists they streamed, weighted by play counts. The 0.75¢-per-stream ceiling stops one play from grabbing a whole wallet, and proration kicks in for heavy listeners so a wallet can never overdraw.

PHX's 0.75¢ is roughly 2× Spotify's per-stream rate — but the real difference is concentration: a Spotify stream splits across 100M tracks; a PHX member's wallet splits across only the handful of local artists they actually play.

Worked example — one Native member, one month

Member pays $15 → wallet $7.50. They played 200 counted streams: 172 Murkemz, 18 Futuristic, 10 Mega Ran.

rate = MIN(0.75¢, $7.50 ÷ 200 = 3.75¢) = 0.75¢ (cap wins — light listener) Murkemz 172 × 0.75¢ = $1.29 Futuristic 18 × 0.75¢ = $0.14 Mega Ran 10 × 0.75¢ = $0.08 ─────────────────────────────── drawn $1.50 leftover → pot $6.00 (funds free-tier plays + platform)

If the same member had played 2,000 streams: only 1,000 count (monthly cap), rate = $7.50 ÷ 1,000 = 0.75¢ — wallet fully drains, farms gain nothing past the cap.

⚠ One thing to decide: the Insider gap

Insider wallets can never fully drain under current settings. Wallet is $14.50, but 1,000 counted streams × 0.75¢ caps the maximum draw at $7.50 — so at least $7.00 of every Insider's wallet always goes to the pot, not to the artists they streamed. That's fine if the intent is "Insiders feed the community pot harder." But the old marketing said Insiders send more to their artists. If that's the intent, make the rate cap tier-aware: cap = wallet ÷ 1000 → Native 0.75¢ (unchanged), Insider 1.45¢. One-line change in payout_settings/engine — say the word and it's applied.

Verify a real period (run in Supabase SQL editor)

After the engine runs, these recompute the numbers independently — they should match payout_periods / artist_payouts to the cent.

-- 1. Expected wallet + drawn per subscriber for a period (dry run, writes nothing)
WITH s AS (SELECT * FROM payout_settings WHERE id=1),
subs AS (
  SELECT sub.user_id, sub.amount_cents,
         sub.amount_cents*(100-s.platform_share_pct)/100.0 AS wallet
  FROM subscriptions sub, s
  WHERE sub.status='active'
    AND sub.current_period_start < DATE '2026-09-01'
    AND sub.current_period_end   > DATE '2026-08-01'),
st AS (
  SELECT user_id, COUNT(*) AS n FROM stream_events
  WHERE counted AND listened_at >= '2026-08-01' AND listened_at < '2026-09-01'
  GROUP BY user_id)
SELECT subs.user_id, wallet/100 AS wallet_usd, COALESCE(st.n,0) AS streams,
       LEAST(s.stream_rate_cap_cents, wallet/NULLIF(st.n,0)) AS rate_cents,
       ROUND(COALESCE(LEAST(s.stream_rate_cap_cents, wallet/st.n)*st.n,0))/100 AS drawn_usd,
       ROUND(wallet - COALESCE(LEAST(s.stream_rate_cap_cents, wallet/st.n)*st.n,0))/100 AS to_pot_usd
FROM subs LEFT JOIN st USING (user_id), s;

-- 2. Engine's answer for the same period
SELECT period_start, total_subscription_revenue_cents/100.0 AS gross,
       streams_paid_cents/100.0 AS paid_to_artists,
       pot_total_cents/100.0 AS pot, pot_free_tier_cents/100.0 AS free_fund
FROM payout_periods ORDER BY period_start DESC LIMIT 3;

-- 3. Per-artist totals the artists will see
SELECT a.name, ap.total_streams, ap.unique_listeners,
       ap.total_earnings_cents/100.0 AS earnings_usd, ap.per_stream_rate_cents
FROM artist_payouts ap JOIN artists a ON a.id=ap.artist_id
ORDER BY earnings_usd DESC;
